Privacy Policy for Easy Write Ai

Effective Date: 12/09/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Easy Write Ai (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or using Easy Write Ai, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Who We Are

Easy Write Ai is a mobile productivity application that uses artificial intelligence to help users draft everyday personal, legal-style, and business documents — such as leave applications, legal notices, rent agreements, RTI applications, complaint letters, invoices, business proposals, and emails.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the App, personalise your experience, and provide AI-generated document drafting. We categorise this information as follows:

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A unique Firebase account identifier (UID)

We use Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to manage sign-in securely. We do not receive or store your account password in plain text — authentication is handled entirely by Firebase’s secure infrastructure.

2.2 Content You Provide

To generate documents, we collect and process:

  • Prompts and form input you type into the App
  • Files and images you choose to upload (e.g. documents to summarise/translate, business cards to scan, photos for OCR)
  • Generated documents and drafts, so you can view, edit, and re-access your history inside the App
  • Personal organisational data you choose to save in the App’s productivity features (tasks, deadlines, notes, reminders)

2.3 Camera, Microphone, and Storage Data

  • Camera: Accessed only when you actively choose to scan a document, business card, or capture an image for a tool that requires it. We do not access the camera in the background.
  • Microphone / Voice Dictation: When you use the voice-typing feature, your speech is transcribed entirely on your device using on-device speech recognition. Your voice audio is never recorded, uploaded, transmitted to, or stored on any server — it exists only transiently on your device during transcription.
  • Storage / File access: Accessed only when you explicitly choose to upload, save, or export a file (e.g. saving a generated PDF, picking a file to summarise).

2.4 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, certain technical information may be collected automatically:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID, device model, operating system version)
  • App usage and interaction data (e.g. which tools you use, session length, crash logs)
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from network connection, for security and fraud-prevention purposes only)
  • Log data related to errors or crashes, for stability and debugging purposes

2.5 Advertising Data

We use Google AdMob to display rewarded video advertisements that let you earn additional free credits. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information and general usage/interaction data with ads
  • Approximate location data (for ad relevance, where permitted)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google’s own Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google AdMob’s policies. We do not control how Google uses this data beyond serving ads within our App. You can review and manage your ad personalisation settings from your device’s Google Settings at any time.

2.6 Payment Information

If you purchase a premium subscription or credit pack, payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see, process, or store your payment card details, UPI details, or any other financial instrument information. Google’s own privacy and payment terms apply to those transactions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

  1. Create and manage your account, and authenticate you securely.
  2. Generate the documents, summaries, translations, and other AI outputs you request.
  3. Save your document history, personal tasks, deadlines, and workspace data so you can access them across sessions.
  4. Sync your data and credit balance in real time across your devices when you are signed in.
  5. Operate the credits and rewarded-ad system (grant free credits, track daily fair-use limits, detect fraud).
  6. Process subscription and credit-pack purchases through Google Play Billing.
  7. Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App (crash reporting, performance monitoring).
  8. Respond to your support requests and communicate important service updates.
  9. Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not use your prompts, uploaded files, or generated documents to train any third-party or public AI model, and we do not sell your personal data to any third party.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share information only with the following categories of service providers, each acting under their own strict data-processing and confidentiality obligations, and only to the extent necessary to operate the App:

Recipient Purpose Data Shared
Firebase (Google) — Authentication, Firestore Database Account management, secure data storage/sync Account identifier, email, profile data, document/workspace data
AI Generation Provider (backend-routed) Generating your requested document/text output The prompt text and any file/image content you submit for that specific request

Important notes on AI processing:

  • Prompts and any uploaded media you submit for a generation request are transmitted, over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection, through our backend to our AI generation provider solely to produce the output you requested.
  • Uploaded media bytes are processed only for that single request and are not permanently stored by our backend once the response is returned to you.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers directly, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data for marketing purposes to any third party.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or ourselves.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Encryption in transit: All communication between the App and our backend uses TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Authentication security: Sign-in tokens are verified server-side. API secrets and provider credentials are kept securely on our backend and are never embedded in or accessible from the App itself.
  • Secure local storage: Sensitive tokens stored on your device use secure, encrypted local storage mechanisms.
  • Data isolation: Your workspace data, documents, and personal records are isolated per signed-in account and are not accessible to other users.
  • Retention: We retain your account and content data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the App’s features (e.g. document history). If you delete your account, associated data is permanently removed as described in Section 6, except where retention is required by law (e.g. certain billing/transaction records).
  • Backups: Limited backups may be retained for a short period for disaster-recovery purposes, after which they are purged on a rolling basis.

While we take reasonable and industry-standard measures to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following controls, available directly inside the App:

  • Access and review: View your document history, saved tasks, and profile information inside the App at any time.
  • Edit and delete individual items: Remove specific documents, tasks, or deadlines from your workspace.
  • Account deletion: Use the “Delete Account” option in your Profile settings to immediately and permanently erase:
    • Your backend profile and account record
    • Your document generation history
    • Your saved personal tasks, deadlines, and workspace data
    • Locally cached app data on your device
    • Your Firebase authentication credentials

    This action is irreversible. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered by us.

  • Ad personalisation: Manage or opt out of personalised advertising through your device’s Google Settings (“Ads” section) at any time — this is independent of your in-app account.
  • Permissions: You can revoke Camera, Microphone, or Storage permissions at any time from your device’s system Settings. Doing so may limit specific features (e.g. document scanning, voice typing) but will not prevent you from using the rest of the App.

If you are located in a jurisdiction with statutory data-protection rights (such as the EU/EEA under GDPR, or India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may additionally have the right to request a copy of your data, request correction of inaccurate data, object to certain processing, or lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. To exercise any of these rights beyond what is available in-app, contact us using the details in Section 1.
